Overview

Simplifying heritage retrofit for churches.

The project explores how service design can make heritage retrofit easier to understand and navigate for churches with limited time, capacity and technical knowledge.

My role
User research, stakeholder mapping, funding and policy research, journey mapping, service strategy and concept development.
Research base
Interviews with clergy, church users and heritage professionals, supported by site visits, policy research and funding analysis.
Outcome
A 10-meeting retrofit toolkit, stakeholder and journey maps, and a simplified route through funding, permissions and project planning.

Persona

In smaller churches, retrofit knowledge is often held by only one key person. Because the process depends on clergy, PCC members, volunteers and external specialists, unclear role division can quickly make early decisions harder. These personas show how different participants enter the same project with different motivations, capacities and needs.
